[p1]
Islands

[p2]
The rum got the best
of me again! To win
my next bet I can’t
encircle other islands.
▪ For each coordinate, find the shortest
sea route encircling the given island.
▪ Your route must not encircle any other
island.

[p3]
Checklist for routes:
▪ Must not visit any tile that is land.
▪ Must not have gaps.
▪ Must not visit the same tile twice.
▪ Must not have two diagonal steps crossing each other.
▪ Must end on one of the 8 neighbor tiles of tile where it started.
▪ Must be the shortest possible route.
▪ Must not encircle any other island.
