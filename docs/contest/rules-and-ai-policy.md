# Rules and AI policy

## Official rules (2026)
Source: https://codingcontest.org/info/how-it-works
- **Languages:** any; problems are in English. "Any language, any tool." (game client)
- **Downloads:** "On-site participants are not allowed to perform excessive downloads or stream content during the contest."
- **Laptops and submitting:** "Teams may use multiple laptops, and every team member can submit solutions on behalf of the team."
- **Terms:** minimum age 16 (14 in Austria), and **one account per person**. **Venue-specific rules emailed before the event take precedence.** https://codingcontest.org/terms/overview

## AI/LLM policy
**No official text explicitly allows or bans AI.** The verifier searched the full platform bundle, the game client, the Terms and the cloudflight.io pages ✅. No rule restricts internet use either, apart from the on-site downloads/streaming line.

Evidence that AI use is tolerated or expected:
- **2024 survey:** Cloudflight's own survey of the April 2024 contest openly reports that 741 participants used or considered AI, versus 299 who didn't. It found AI users >10% slower on average. https://www.cloudflight.io/en/blog/ai-assisted-software-development-strengths-and-negative-usecases/
- **2026 LinkedIn posts:** "#AI is changing everything, including how we code and compete in 2026…" and "A magic wand is nothing without the magician… The same is true for AI". https://linkedin.com/showcase/cloudflight-coding-contest
- **2024 participant blog:** "AI tools that were allowed during the competition". https://nikity.is-a.dev/blog/blog-1.html
- **2025 team names:** the top two teams were "KI_ist_unverdient" and "ai_usage_is_no_fun".
- ❌ The Codeforces "…and AI" headline is **not** evidence; it refers to the Data/AI track.
